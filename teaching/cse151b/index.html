CSE 151B: Deep Learning

Schedule Assignments Grading Project Office Hours Readings & Videos

An introduction to deep learning: how the major architectures work, why they were built, and how to reason about their behavior. The course runs a flipped classroom with pre-lecture preparation, in-class problem solving, oral assessments, and a project. This syllabus is subject to change.

Learning outcomes

Understand the major architectures, the processes that drive them, and the broad shape of advanced topics.

  • Architectures: multilayer perceptron (MLP), convolutional neural networks (CNN), recurrent neural networks (RNN), transformers
  • Processes and algorithms: activation functions (ReLU), backpropagation, attention
  • Basic familiarity with advanced topics: reinforcement learning with human feedback (RLHF), large language models (LLMs), and additional topics to be finalized

For each topic, the aim is to know how and why it arose, its affordances and drawbacks relative to alternatives, how its structure produces those properties, and how it responds to different inputs and data distributions. See the syllabus for the full attributes-of-understanding table.
