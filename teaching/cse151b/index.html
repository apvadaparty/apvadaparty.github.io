Learning outcomes

Understand the major architectures, the processes that drive them, and the broad shape of advanced topics.

  • Architectures: multilayer perceptron (MLP), convolutional neural networks (CNN), recurrent neural networks (RNN) and long short-term memory (LSTM), transformers
  • Processes and algorithms: activation functions (ReLU), backpropagation, attention
  • Basic familiarity with advanced topics: reinforcement learning with human feedback (RLHF), large language models (LLMs)

For each topic, the aim is to know how and why it arose, its affordances and drawbacks relative to alternatives, how its structure produces those properties, and how it responds to different inputs and data distributions. See the syllabus for the full attributes-of-understanding table.

Teaching philosophy

This course is guided by pragmatism, joy, and equity. We design for real students with real constraints: workloads are meaningful but manageable, the classroom is flipped so you learn at your own pace and we use class time for the hard parts, and mastery learning is built in through retakes at each step. We also center joy — good teaching builds a bridge from where you are to the new ideas and helps you appreciate what makes them worth knowing. And we work to keep the playing field level: flex days, dropped scores, and free compute resources ensure that circumstances outside your control don’t determine your outcome.
