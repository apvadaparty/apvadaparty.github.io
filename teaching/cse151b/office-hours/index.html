Asking for help

There are several ways to get help in this course:

  • In class and discussion. Ask questions during lecture and during the Monday/Wednesday discussion sections.
  • Office hours. Come to group or one-on-one office hours with the instructor or teaching staff.
  • Piazza, publicly. Post questions publicly so the whole class benefits from the answer. Please do not post solutions or answers to assignments; if your question would reveal one, make it private or bring it to office hours.

If you are unsure where to start, or you are stuck and not sure how to ask, reach out to the instructor — that is what we are here for.
